The first thought that came to mind when I saw the post was — why not build something fun? 😄

We love food. ❤️
So why not build something useless enough… but fun enough? 😂

🥚 The Idea

Egg puffs! Something we absolutely love eating. 🤤

But here's the catch — for every egg puff you eat, you only get half an egg. 🥚➗2

Where does the other half go? 🤔

To some stranger… somewhere. 👀

And the funniest part? We both share a single egg without even knowing it. 😂

So we thought…

Why not build something that connects these two people? 🫂

And then came the obvious question… HOW?! 😵‍💫

Brainstorming mode: ON. 🧠💡

Then came the idea 💡:

What if we put an ice cream stick with a unique code on every egg puff? 🍦🥚

Each code would have a pair, and the stick would be placed on the puff while it was being made.

So somewhere out there, another person would have the other half of your egg. 👀

And just like that… Eggmates was born. 🥚❤️
